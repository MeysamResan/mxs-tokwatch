# Local project instructions

Do not upload, commit, or publish anything. Those actions are performed manually by the owner only.

This is a native Windows 11 Rust/Win32 tray application. Preserve its small footprint, event-driven interface, and read-only Codex account monitoring. Authentication is managed by the official Codex helper.

Use scripts/build.ps1 for formatting, linting, tests, and local release builds. Do not add publishing or release-upload workflows. Keep .tools, target, dist, and .verification out of source control.
